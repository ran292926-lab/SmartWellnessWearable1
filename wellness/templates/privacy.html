Privacy notice

What Smart Wellness Wearable collects, why, who sees it, how long it is kept and your rights.

Who we are

Smart Wellness Wearable is a student prototype built for the SW 341 course by Group G3. It processes your data only with your consent, which you give when you tick the box on the registration form. All data stays on the application server; it is not sold, used for advertising or sent outside the server.

How we protect it

  • All traffic is encrypted (TLS).
  • Readings, alerts, birth year and weight are stored encrypted (AES-256).
  • Every request for another person's data is checked against what they chose to share.
  • Nothing is shared with a contact until they accept and you select a category.

Your rights

  • Be informed: this notice.
  • Access and copy: My data shows all your data, and you can download it as a JSON file.
  • Correction: change your name, email, password, birth year and weight in My account.
  • Deletion: delete your account in My account; your data is deleted at once.
  • Withdraw sharing: remove a contact or a category in Trusted contacts; it applies from the next request.
