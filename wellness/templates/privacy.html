Privacy notice

What Sanad collects, why, who sees it, how long it is kept and your rights.

Who we are

Sanad (Smart Wellness Wearable) is a student prototype built for the SW 341 course by Group G3. It processes your data only with your consent, which you give when you tick the box on the registration form. You can download your own data. Trusted contacts can view only the categories you share with them. Your data is not sold or used for advertising.

How we protect it

  • All traffic is encrypted (TLS).
  • Readings, alerts, birth year and weight are stored encrypted (AES-256).
  • Every request for another person's data is checked against what they chose to share.
  • An invitation shows your display name to the invited person. Your health data is shared only after they accept and you select a category.

Your rights

  • Be informed: this notice.
  • Access and copy: My data shows your profile, account and security data, contacts, readings and alerts, and you can download them as a JSON file. Passwords, tokens and session identifiers are stored only in protected form and are never shown.
  • Correction: change your name, email, password, birth year and weight in My account.
  • Deletion: delete your account in My account; your data is deleted at once.
  • Withdraw sharing: remove a contact or a category in Trusted contacts; it applies from the next request.
